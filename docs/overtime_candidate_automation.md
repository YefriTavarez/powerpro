# Overtime Candidate automation

## Purpose

Surface plausible overtime from existing `Employee Checkin` evidence so a
supervisor can approve Cash, approve Compensatory Rest, reject the candidate,
or mark the check-in evidence invalid. A candidate and its decision are audit
records only: this phase does not create `Overtime Authorization`,
`Retroactive Overtime Adjustment`, `Additional Salary`, `Leave Allocation`,
`Salary Slip`, Journal Entry, or any other payroll/accounting document.

## Detection rules

- Active employees already marked `Overtime Eligible` are evaluated.
- Active employees whose Designation matches a configured plant keyword are
  evaluated and surfaced as `Eligibility Pending` until eligibility and an
  approver are configured.
- Work evidence on a legal holiday is always evaluated, including a legal
  holiday that falls on the weekly rest day.
- A weekly rest day by itself is not automatically treated as overtime.
- On a regular workday, the last valid OUT must be at least the configured
  threshold after the scheduled shift end. The default threshold is 15 minutes.
- Exact duplicate punches are ignored. Missing or ambiguous IN/OUT pairs are
  visible as `Needs Check-in Review`; the system does not invent hours.
- Day shifts stop at midnight. Overnight shifts may use the following day's
  final OUT before the next shift occurrence.
- An existing non-cancelled `Overtime Authorization` or
  `Retroactive Overtime Adjustment` prevents a duplicate candidate. A
  previously open candidate is marked `Superseded`.

## Idempotency and audit behavior

The unique key is `Employee + Work Date`. Repeated scans update only Open,
Eligibility Pending, or Needs Check-in Review records. A supervisor's final
decision is never overwritten by the scheduler. The saved record includes the
employee/shift/holiday snapshot, candidate intervals, source check-ins,
warnings, an evidence hash, the decision reason, user, and timestamp.

## HR correction workflow

Users with HR Manager, Manufacturing Manager, or System Manager can open the
`Overtime Candidate` list and use **Overtime Tools > Refresh from Check-ins**.
The dialog requires a Company, accepts an explicit date range of up to 32
calendar days, and always shows a read-only preview before asking for
confirmation. Today and future dates are rejected; an overnight shift is not
treated as fully evaluated until its scheduled end has passed.

The confirmed refresh creates or updates only reviewable candidates. If a
successfully evaluated employee/date no longer has a qualifying overtime signal
after the check-ins are corrected, the reviewable candidate is marked
`Invalid Check-in` with an audit reason, user, and timestamp. A final supervisor
decision is never overwritten. A candidate linked to an existing non-cancelled
overtime record is superseded instead.

Scheduled generation does not perform stale-candidate invalidation. That action
is restricted to the explicit, previewed HR workflow.

## Safe rollout sequence

1. Deploy the app update. The new feature flag defaults to disabled, so no
   candidates are generated by deployment alone.
2. Verify `Overtime Candidate` and the new fields in `DGII Payroll Settings`
   exist. Confirm `Enable Overtime Candidate Generation` is off.
3. In DEV, use `Preview Overtime Candidates`. Preview is read-only.
4. Review the candidate count and a representative regular day, legal holiday,
   incomplete punch pair, and overnight shift.
5. Enable the feature in DEV and use `Generate Overtime Candidates Now`.
   Re-run it and confirm the second pass creates no duplicates.
6. Test each decision. Confirm the result reports zero payroll documents and
   zero leave documents created.
7. Only after DEV acceptance, repeat the deployment verification in production,
   preview first, and obtain separate approval before enabling the production
   feature flag.

## Expected production state immediately after deployment

- `Enable Overtime Candidate Generation`: disabled.
- Scheduled job: registered but exits without reading attendance or writing
  candidates while the flag is disabled.
- Existing employees, shifts, check-ins, overtime records, payroll, leave,
  accounting, and the historical Excel workbook: unchanged.

## Rollback

Immediate operational rollback is to disable `Enable Overtime Candidate
Generation`. This stops new scans while preserving the audit records already
created. If code rollback is required, deploy the prior PowerPro commit after
disabling the flag. Do not delete candidate records as part of routine rollback;
they are audit evidence and have no payroll or accounting effect.

## Production risks

- Incorrect shift assignments or Holiday Lists can create false positives; the
  evidence and warnings must be reviewed before approval.
- Designation keywords intentionally cast a broad plant-employee net. Those
  employees stay `Eligibility Pending` until explicitly configured.
- An approval in this phase records the supervisor's decision only. A later,
  separately approved integration is required to create cash earnings or a
  compensatory leave allocation automatically.
